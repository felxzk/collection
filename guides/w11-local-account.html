Creating a Local Account on Windows 11

    microsoft, in recent months, has made this ever more difficult to accomplish. however, it still remains possible.

  • while in the OOBE setup, once you've reached the screen prompting for your country, press Shift+F10.
  • a cmd window should appear. input the command OOBE\BYPASSNRO and your machine should reboot. this removes the requirement of an online account in the windows setup.
  • once rebooted, hit Shift+F10 once more, and input ipconfig /release , then press enter. this will, in a sense, dislocate your internet so that it is temporarily unavailable on this machine.
  • continue with installation, and if you are presented with a screen asking you to connect to the internet, select the option that reads 'I don't have internet', then 'continue with limited setup'.

that's a lot of work...thankfully, there's another way out of this!

  • download rufus, and download/import the windows 11 iso.
  • once this is imported, click 'start', and you should be presented a dialog box with a few options.
  • in these options, there is one to remove the requirement for an online account, and if you want to go a step further, there's one directly below it which pre-creates the local account for you.
